# Changelog

Short, plain lines. Each version is one playable sample page in `play/`.

## v7.03
- Grudge meter, the first of the v7 changes. Chase Start and Turbo Lap no longer stack. Test build: passed robot races only.

## v6.31
- Free races have three goals to chase, shown during the countdown. Each goal met adds fans and coins.
- Finishing fourth pays a small consolation.

## v6.30
- Road surfaces and road conditions fade in and out, with no hard seams.
- Hard rivals' road effects fade in too.

## v6.29
- A flick no longer steers the car for a moment.
- The results show thumb lean and reach figures.
- Roads are a little darker again.

## v6.28
- Hits briefly freeze the frame.
- Reduce shake setting for a calmer camera.

## v6.27
- Crash Cup: a derby on Skyway Eight, unlocked by finishing a race there. Bumps dent, smoke and total the car. Fans come from bumps and rams.

## v6.26
- The flat top-down view is gone. The tilted view is the only view.

## v6.25
- Day to dusk is the default time of day.

## v6.24
- New time of day option, Day to dusk. The race goes from day to dusk, and the headlights turn on near the end.

## v6.23
- Soft grass and dirt patches on the ground, and tree clusters on the outside of corners.

## v6.22
- Off-road spots are printed in the results report, and dirt shortcuts count toward skill seconds.
- A quick second touch keeps the steering anchor, so a re-touch no longer jumps the car.
- How to play has 14 picture cards. Settings rows have icons and one-line descriptions.

## v6.21
- One landmark per theme sits in the infield.
- Road colors are darker in every theme, so the road stands out from the grass.

## v6.20
- Rivals behind you catch up a little, and rivals ahead no longer slow down.
- Skill seconds: drift boosts, shortcuts and fence hits show a small estimate in the results.
- Drivers in the cars have helmets and shoulders. The roof is off the hatch, sedan, sports car and truck.

## v6.13
- Autopilot is less: the car no longer follows the road by itself when the thumb is down.
- The loop-de-loop only takes you in when you are lined up with it.
- The camera shows more of the track ahead. Boost trail is thinner.
- Garage cars have bumpers, grilles, plates and mirrors.

## v6.12
- Quick tour on first visit, and again from How to play.
- Tracks show a map, and upgrades show an icon.

## v6.11
- Boost is coasting when you keep the thumb up, and rivals catch up less.
- Upgrades and Tracks moved to the big row on the title screen.
- Sparks, dust and flame effects are sharper.

## v6.10
- Steering with road help off follows the bend more, and the car straightens up after a hit.
- Ramps can skip a barrier when the landing is close to the road.
- Position is a big gold number. The coin count has a coin picture.

## v6.04
- The Boost button is gone. Boost is a flick up, or the space bar. Boost meters sit under the speedometer.

## v6.03
- Flick up while steering to boost.
- With road help off, the car follows the bend while the thumb is up or near the middle.

## v6.02
- Trace line in Copy results: a short record of side, thumb, speed and drift state, for testing.
- Corner braking also works off the road.

## v6.01
- Corner braking with road help off, and more grip off the road so the car can steer back.

## v6.00
- Campaign "Road to the Big Top": six towns, each with three races and a duel. Duel wins earn vehicles.
- Fancy is half size and flies across the top of the screen.
- Steering with road help off changed. Still being tuned.

## v5.33
- Underdog Rocket: a pink bar above Boost fills faster when you are behind. When it is full, double-tap Boost for a short rocket burst.

## v5.32
- Eighth track, Big Top Loop: a circus track with a loop-de-loop. Cars ride through it, and rivals do too. Opens at level 1 for now.

## v5.31
- Bridge shadows, cars under the bridge show as outlines, and coins sit on the bridge.

## v5.30
- Fancy is redrawn to look like he does in his own game: a curved banana with big eyes, a red mask, a red scarf with two waving tails, rosy cheeks and a smile. He still flies across the screen now and then on the beach and candy tracks.
- Slide steering: the invisible center point now follows your thumb when you slide past full reach, so turning back the other way answers right away.
- Slide steering with road help off: a small dead zone around the center keeps the car straight when your thumb wobbles a little.
- Slide steering with road help off: a drift now starts only when you push the thumb most of the way and hold it a little longer, so small corrections no longer turn into slides.
- Monster truck steers a little sharper.
- The line under the logo on the title page now reads "Tiny cars. Big drifts. One thumb."

## v5.29
- New track, Skyway Eight (level 3): a figure eight where the road climbs a ramp onto a bridge and crosses over itself. The bridge has rails, and cars on different levels do not touch.
- On Easy rivals, level 1 of every upgrade is free.

## v5.28
- Five new vehicles in the Garage: Motorbike (450 coins, level 4), Three-wheeler (500 coins, level 5), Minivan with a rocket on the roof (900 coins, level 7), Formula racer (2,500 coins, level 9) and Robotruck (5,000 coins, bad at everything).
- Road conditions on Hard rivals: wet road and mud, deep sand on the beach, spilled soda and sticky syrup on Candy Ridge, black ice and snow drifts on Glacier Party. Slippery stretches cut grip and sticky ones cut speed. Rivals slow a little on them too, and the race report shows the time spent on them.
- The view pulls back as you speed up, and a little more on boost. Speed lines now start at normal top speed.
- New setting "Thumb reach" for slide steering: Short, Medium or Long. A longer reach makes the steering calmer.
- With road help off, the thumb reach is a little longer and the car catches big slides at part strength.

## v5.27
- Normal rivals pulled back halfway between v5.24 and v5.25.

## v5.26
- A striped speed bump across the whole road on Orchard Ring. It costs about a fifth of your speed and gives a small hop. Rivals slow for it too, and the race report counts it.
- Prince Chimp watches from a tiki throne beside Banana Beach, and a chimp submarine circles the lake there.
- Gulls circle in a corner of the screen on parts of the beach and ice tracks.
- Penguins and pineapple characters now face the camera.

## v5.25
- Normal rivals are a little faster and chase harder when you lead, more so with road help on.
- The Done button stays pinned at the bottom of every menu.
- People now fly a kite, wave a bigger flag, hold balloons and a sign, eat ice cream on the beach and candy tracks, and wear winter clothes on the ice track, next to a snowman.
- Penguins and pineapple characters on Glacier Party, and apple piles and baskets on Orchard Ring.
- Fancy flies across the screen now and then on the beach and candy tracks.

## v5.24
- People are redrawn with legs, shoes, arms and shaped bodies, and are a little larger.
- Road surfaces change by section: asphalt, cobblestone, concrete slabs and packed dirt. Looks only, grip is unchanged.
- Wider stripes on the cars.
- Monster truck, earned at 200 lifetime fans, and six paint looks earned from 25 to 1,000 lifetime fans. The garage shows the fan count.

## v5.23
- Four themed tracks (an apple orchard, a beach, a candy shop and a glacier party), six tracks in all, with rubber and burn marks on the road, new driver faces, and less shiny cars.
- Since v5.17: Easy, Normal and Hard rivals, solid crowds, fences that slide you forward, fan points, a hand-picked paint list, and a fuller race report.

## v5.22
- Boost tuning: the meter fills more slowly, drifts pay fans instead of extra boost, and the Boost button uses the meter.
- Ramp jumps go a bit more than twice as high at the same length.

## v5.21
- Hitting a wall now slides you along it. Fences are solid only near the track, and people near shortcuts are removed.

## v5.20
- Rivals on Easy, Normal and Hard. The car can no longer drive over people. Bouncy edges and turn speed cost are settings. A paint picker with 24 paints and 10 ready-made looks, and a fuller results report.
- People got hair, hats and skin tones on the same simple bodies. The full redraw came in v5.24.

## v5.19
- "Copy last results" now copies your last race, not the title demo.

## v5.18
- Turbo and tire upgrades add more drift boost. The Boost button glows at each drift tier.

## v5.17
- Assist-off bonus (1.5x), a bigger color palette, photographers along the track, a drift look, and a wider view on larger screens.

## v5.16
- Darker dusk, two headlights per car, and keyboard steering with arrow keys and space for Boost.

## v5.15
- Levels and track unlocks: experience from every race, new cars, drivers and tracks as you level up.

## v5.14
- Second track (Twin Pines), car paint and stripe colors, and a big coin worth 5.

## v5.13
- Wallet, upgrades, cars for sale, drivers with faces, and standings.

## v5.12
- Close-call bonus, the final-lap hunter rival, a slow-motion photo finish, flying cones and tires, and chain flips.

## v5.11
- Jump ramps, speed pads, oil slicks, puddles, 3D bonuses, and a rocket start.

## v5.10
- Fence, grandstands with a crowd, track signs, and a start gantry.

## v5.9
- Car wear, a smaller lap banner, a fix to road help, and slipstream.

## v5.2 to v5.8
