# Changelog

Short, plain lines. Each version is one playable sample page in `play/`.

## v7.50
- PAINT SWAP! side rubs on (a light side contact costs much less than a BUMP), Shake It Off on (drive clean after a hit to refill some boost, when not in 1st), and the speed gauge and minimap redraw only when they change (faster HUD).

## v7.49
- Side-rub test switch (still off): every second rub on the same rival adds a grudge point.

## v7.48
- Test switch for graded side rubs (off): a light side contact becomes a short RUB instead of a BUMP.

## v7.47
- Fair grudge shove (a grudge rival bumps no harder than others), Closing In pays +2 when the car ahead holds a grudge of 2 or more, and Podium Pop shows only when you finish top 3.

## v7.46
- Sticker Book: 8 stickers per Road to the Big Top town (win, RAZOR!, flip, last-bend pass, rematch win, saved photo, 3 stars, town duel); locked ones show ?; a full page unlocks that town's paint; Stickers button on the title. Test knob for the grudge shove (unchanged).

## v7.45
- Podium Pop: when a race ends the top three pop onto a podium (3rd, 2nd, 1st, 0.4 s apart) with a pose each; at 4th or lower your driver shrugs. Tap to skip. The photo and prize box wait for it.

## v7.44
- Race Headline: the results card NEWS line is written from your race (rematch win, final-bend pass, a rival you flipped, a RAZOR! pass) and names the rival; otherwise the old random jokes. Test knob for a Closing In grudge bonus (off).

## v7.43

## v7.42

## v7.41

## v7.40

## v7.39

## v7.38
