Settings

Driving

SteeringHow your thumb steers the car.
Thumb reachLonger reach makes slide steering calmer and easier to hold steady.
Road helpThe car straightens along the road when you let go. Turn it off to earn 1.5x coins and fans.
Hands-off helpWith road help off, how much the car still steers itself when you lift or hold the thumb centered. None is fully manual.
Look-ahead aidsZooms out a little when you lift your thumb or slide sideways, and points an arrow at the screen edge when the next bend is off screen.

Race rules

RivalsHow fast the other cars drive. Faster rivals pay more coins and XP for a top 3 finish. A rival far behind you gets a small push; Hard has no help and adds wet, icy, muddy or sticky stretches.
Turns cost speedHard steering scrubs off speed. A clean drift keeps more of it.
Bouncy edgesFences and barriers spring you back instead of stopping you.
Chase startStart the race a few seconds behind everyone. Every fan you win is worth more, and the whole race becomes passing. Not used in the campaign.
Turbo LapThe last lap: everyone drives 8% faster and every fan counts 1.5 times.
Friendly rivalsA rival that bumps you hard hops aside and says sorry. A rival far ahead may show off and lose a little time. Never when you are the one far ahead.
Crash Cup (Skyway Eight only)Crashes earn big fans. Too many hits and your car is totaled, with a repair bill of 120 coins. Finish a race on Skyway Eight to unlock it.

Screen and comfort

Time of dayDay, sunset or dusk. Big Top Overnight is the one track that slides from day into night.
Reduce shakeCalms the screen shake on bumps and crashes. Turn it on if the shaking bothers you.
Night headlight streaksAt night, your headlights flare into short streaks while you boost.
Drift ribbonsWhile you drift, your back wheels leave short glowing ribbons in your paint color. They fade in about a second.
Open-road wind (try it)Whoever leads alone, well clear of second place, slowly tires (up to 3% slower), shown by wind lines in front. Tuck in behind a car to clear it. Rivals tire too.
One dial for speed and boostThe boost meter is the outer ring of the speedometer, so there is less on the right side of the screen. It glows when a boost is ready.
Speed linesWhen you go fast or boost, the view widens a little and thin lines streak in at the screen edges. Reduce shake keeps them small.
Crash slow motionWhen you flip a rival, the screen blurs at the top and bottom and slows down for about a second. Tap to skip. Reduce shake turns it off.
Finish photoYour finish turns into an instant photo on the results card. Save photo keeps a copy on this device.
Glowing bend edges (night)At dusk and night, the edges of bends glow warm white so you can see the curve coming. Straights stay dark.
Moving colorsWater shimmers, Candy Ridge curbs flow through candy colors, and Big Top Overnight curbs light up like marquee bulbs after dark. WebGL only.
GraphicsWebGL2 draws with the graphics chip and looks best. Canvas is the safe fallback if the screen flickers or stays blank.
VibrationShort buzzes on bumps, boosts and level ups, on phones that support it.

How to play

Clean boostFlick right after a drift ends, or when the car points down the road right after a turn, for a boost 1.4 times longer. Saving the meter makes any boost longer.
Grip warningLight smoke and faint marks mean the tires are near their limit. Ease off before the slide.
Turbo LapOn the last lap everyone drives 8 percent faster and fans count 1.5 times. With Chase Start on, only you speed up.
Grudges and arrowsBump a rival and it remembers, up to 6 bumps, and shoves back harder. Colored arrows at the screen edge show a rival catching up from behind and the next sharp bend.
Chase StartIn Settings, start 2, 3 or 6 seconds behind. Every fan counts more and the whole race is passing.
Look-ahead aidsLifting your thumb or sliding sideways zooms the view out a little, and the minimap fades when the road runs under it. Turn it off in Settings.
Flick up to boostKeep your thumb down and flick up. The orange bar is your boost.
Thumb up is safeWith road help off, how much the car follows the bend by itself depends on Hands-off help in Settings. It never pulls you to the middle.
Underdog RocketThe pink bar fills when you are behind or drifting. When it glows, flick up.
Rocket startFlick up the moment the lights turn green. Too early and you lose it.
Steer with one thumbSlide left or right anywhere, or pick Drag a target in Settings.
On a keyboardArrows steer, space bar boosts.
Drift to fill the barTurn hard so the car slides, then flick up on the straight.
Grab the risky extrasCoins, fans and boost cans sit near the edges and on dirt shortcuts.
Ram a rivalBoost into a rival to spin it. A plain bump only slows you.
SlipstreamTuck in right behind a rival for extra speed and a little boost.
Ramps and padsRamps give air and coins, pads give free speed. Dodge oil and puddles.
Show offSmash cones, squeeze past rivals, hold long drifts for extra coins.
2 lapsFinish first for the biggest prize.
Level upEvery race gives XP. New levels unlock cars, drivers, tracks and upgrades.
