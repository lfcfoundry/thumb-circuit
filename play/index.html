How to play

  1. Rocket start. Tap Boost the moment the lights turn green (it flashes green) for a free head start. Too early and you lose it.
  2. On a keyboard. Left and right arrows steer, and the space bar is Boost.
  3. Steer with one thumb. Slide left or right anywhere, or switch to Drag a target in Settings.
  4. Drift to fill the Boost button. Turn hard in a corner so the car slides sideways, then tap Boost on the straight.
  5. Grab the risky extras. Coins, fans and boost cans sit near the road edges and on the dirt shortcuts.
  6. Boost into a rival to ram it and send it spinning. A plain bump only slows you down.
  7. Tuck in right behind a rival for a slipstream: extra speed and a little boost.
  8. Hit the striped ramps and arrow pads. Ramps give air and coins, pads give free speed. Dodge oil and puddles.
  9. Show off. Smash cones and tire stacks, squeeze past rivals for a close call, and hold long drifts for extra coins.
  10. 2 laps. Finish first for the biggest prize.
  11. Level up. Every finished race gives experience. New levels unlock cars, drivers, the second track and stronger upgrades. Rivals speed up a little each level.
